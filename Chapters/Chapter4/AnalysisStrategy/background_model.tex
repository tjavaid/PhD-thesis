The dominant and sub-dominant irreducible backgrounds for $\Hllll$ process are the production of ZZ via annihilation of a \qqbar pair and the gg fusion respectively. 
Other background is reducible which is of the instrumental nature and arises from the process where partial jets are misidentified as leptons. The main processes which contributes in this 
background are: production of jets in association with Z boson, production of the jets in association with WZ boson pair and the \TTbar pair production. This background is as Z+X.

\par
For fiducial cross section measurement for $\Hllll$, both of irreducible backgrounds ($\Pq\Paq \to \Z\Z$ and gg~$\to \Z\Z$) are estimated from  Monte Carlo (MC) 
simulation as done earlier in Ref.~\cite{Chatrchyan:2013mxa}. In addition, following the study in Ref.~\cite{Bonvini:2013}, in these measurements the expected yield for the gg~$\to \Z\Z$ background has been increased 
by assigning the same ($m_{4\ell}$ dependent) k-factor as signal $\Hllll$ process to leading order (LO) cross section of background. In the region ($105 < m_{4\ell} < 140 \GeV$) this factor leads to an increase of expected
 gg~$\to~ZZ$ background by factor of $\sim 2.66$. After inclusion of this k-factor, the gg~$\to ZZ$ process is still a sub-dominant background in all measurements.
 \par  
 Reducible background (Z+X) is estimated using data. A tight-to-loose ``fake rate'' technique is used in the control regions of data by following Ref.~\cite{Chatrchyan:2013mxa}. 
 Z+X background yields are determined using two methods:
\begin{itemize}
	\item Opposite Sign (OS) method
	\item Same Sign (SS) method
\end{itemize}
Both methods are found to be consistent  with each other within the statistical uncertainties. 
First control region is attained with the requirement that two leptons do pass loose selections which does fulfill the criteria of $\Z_1$ candidate (failing ID and 
isolation requirements), while the other two leptons in the event passes  the criteria of $\Z_1$ candidate.  Thus, the sample is represented as "2 Prompt + 2 Fail`` (2P2F) which is populated  with the events which contain two prompt leptons (which are mostly Drell-Yan with small fraction of $\Zgam$ and $\TTbar$ events) only. 
Second control region is attained by requiring that three leptons pass the final requirements. It is represented as 
"3 Prompt + 1 Fail`` (3P1F) sample.
\par 
The reducible background's $m_{4\ell}$ shape  is attained from the OS method by performing a fit of $m_{4\ell}$ distributions of 3P1F and 2P2F  events
independently by empirical analytical functional constructed by Landau~\cite{landau:1944} in Ref.~\cite{Chatrchyan:2013mxa}.
Reducible background prediction in all three channels fitted together using this with total uncertainty indicated is shown in Figure ~\ref{fig:ZX}.
%Prediction for the reducible background in all three channels together fitted  using this empirical shape with indicated total uncertainty is shown in 
%Figure~\ref{fig:ZX}. 
For differentiall measurements in $\Hllll$, shape of reducible background is attained from templates built from the control regions in the data for each differential bin of the observable considered for measurement. This procedure is similar to what was used for spin-parity studies as described in the Ref.~\cite{Chatrchyan:2013mxa}.
More details on this procedure
