The event selection for these measurements are similar to the event selection used in previous Higgs boson properties measurements in the same final 
state~~\cite{Chatrchyan:2013mxa, Khachatryan:2014kca}. The online event selection is based on the double lepton  and triple electron triggers.  The minimum
$p_{T}$ of 17 and 8~GeV is required for leading and subleading leptons for double leptons triggers, while 15, 8 and
5~GeV for triple electron trigger.  In offline selection, events are selected with at least four well identified and isolated leptons produced at primary 
vertex. Z$_{1}$ is reconstructed with invariant mass closest to the world average Z boson mass among all possible combinations of same flavor and opposite charge 
(SFOS) pairs of the leptons within mass range of $40 \le m({\mathrm{Z}_1}) \le 120$~GeV. Z$_{2}$ is reconstructed by remaining SFOS pair of leptons within invariant 
mass range of $12 \le m({\mathrm{Z}_2}) \le 120$~GeV. If more than one Z$_{2}$ pass the criteria, the leptons pair  with largest  $\Sigma |\pt|$ is 
selected. There should be at least two leptons with \pt$ \ge 20$ and $ \ge 10 \Gev$ among four selected leptons forming Z$_{1}$ and Z$_{2}$. To remove 
contamination from QCD low masses resonance, any opposite-charge pair of leptons is required to pass $m({\ell^{+}\ell'^{-}}) \ge 4 \GeV$. The final state
radiated (FSR) photons are  also recovered and briefly discussed in Section \ref{sec:FSRphotons}.
\par Reconstruction of the jets is  incumbent to explore the differential cross section measurements with respect to jets related observables. Jets 
are reconstructed with $p_T \ge 30$~GeV and $|\eta|\le 4.7$, discussed in Section \ref{jets_reco}. To remove jets overlap with leptons or selected photons, it is
required to have at least   $\Delta R \equiv \sqrt{(\Delta \eta)^2+ (\Delta \phi)^2} > 0.5$~\cite{Chatrchyan:2013mx} from any of the selected leptons and FSR photons candidates.
