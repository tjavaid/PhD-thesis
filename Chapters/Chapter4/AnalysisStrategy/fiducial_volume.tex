The fraction of all $\Hllll$ events that get selected following the event selection procedure can change significantly for various Higgs boson 
production mechanism and distinct exotic models of the Higgs boson. The acceptance difference between SM $\Hllll$ decays and other 
exotic models (Higgs-like spin-one models), processes with large hadronic activity ( like $\ttH$ production ) and different kinematics processes
(like H$\to  {\rm Z}\gamma^{*}(\gamma^{*}\gamma^{*})  \to 4 \ell$ ) can vary up to 70\%.  In order to minimize the dependence of the measurements
on the underlying assumption on the models of the Higgs boson production and properties, the
fiducial phase space for the $\Hllll$ cross section measurements is defined to match as closely as possible the experimental acceptance 
defined by the reconstruction level selection.
\par 
The fiducial phase space demands for at least four leptons having $\pt>7(5) \GeV$  for electrons (muons) with leading and sub-leading leptons
having $\pt>20 \GeV$ and $\pt>10 \GeV$. The four-momenta of each lepton is calculated at the hard scattering level
({\sc pythia} status code $= 3$), before any final state radiation occurs. All electrons (muons) must have pseudorapidity  $|\eta|<2.5(2.4)$.
Each lepton isolation is calculated by adding $\pt$ of all stable particles ({\sc pythia} status code $= 1$) within a cone of radius $\Delta R<0.4$ around 
that lepton. All neutrinos and FSR photons are excluded from above summation. Then the relative isolation of the lepton is defined by the ratio of this
sum and $\pt$ of the considered lepton which is required to be less than $0.4$, consistent with reconstruction level isolation 
requirement~\cite{Chatrchyan:2013mxa}. The requirement of isolation in fiducial phase space definition is crucial to control the  difference of 
signal selection efficiency among different models. The studies based on simulation samples  show that signal efficiency can change up to 
45\% among different models without requiring lepton isolation at fiducial level as shown in Table \ref{tab:efficiencySMnoGenIso}, the maximum difference comes 
from $\ttH$ and $\ggH$ production modes. This behavior is expected as $\ttH$ events has more hadronic activity around leptons as compared to $\ggH$ 
events, so the cut at relative isolation  kills more events in $\ttH$ than $\ggH$.
 \par 
Furthermore, fiducial phase space is defined by several other topological and kinematics selections. There must be two SFOS lepton pairs, 
Z$_{1}$ is reconstructed with invariant mass closest to the world average Z boson mass by all possible combination of same flavor and opposite charge 
(SFOS) pairs of leptons within mass range of $40 \le m({\mathrm{Z}_1}) \le 120$~GeV. Z$_{2}$ is reconstructed by remaining SFOS pair of leptons within inv. 
mass range of $12 \le m({\mathrm{Z}_2}) \le 120$~GeV. If more than one Z$_{2}$ passes the criteria, the leptons pair  with largest  $\Sigma |\pt|$ is 
selected. To remove the overlapping between leptons, we ask for $\Delta R(\ell_{i}\ell_{j})>0.02$ for any $i\neq j$. To remove 
contamination from QCD low masses resonance, any opposite-charge pair of leptons is required to pass $m({\ell^{+}\ell'^{-}}) \ge 4 \GeV$. Finally, 
We require that all selected leptons must emerge from Higgs boson decay and invariant  mass of four-leptons must fulfill  
$105 \GeV < \mathrm{m}(4\ell) < 140 \GeV$. The demand for $\mathrm{m}(4\ell)$ helps to control the contamination from off-shell gg fusion production
which is of considerable size, capable to increase the total cross sextion by a few percent~\cite{Kauer:2013cga, Kauer:2012hd}. The requirements
that defines  fiducial phase space are also gathered in Table~\ref{tab:FidDef}.
