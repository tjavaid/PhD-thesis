 For lepton to be consistent with some common primary vertex, we require that they have an associated track that corresponds a small impact parameter from the event primary vertex.  
We use the significance of impact parameter to the vertex of an event, i.e. $ | {\rm SIP_{3D}} = \frac{\rm IP}{\sigma_{\rm IP}} |$, where ${\rm IP}$ being three dimensional lepton impact parameter w.r.t the primary interaction vertex, and the $\sigma_{\rm IP}$ being the uncertainty associated to this.  Hence, a lepton satisfying $| {\rm SIP_{3D}} | < 4$ is regarded as "primary lepton".
